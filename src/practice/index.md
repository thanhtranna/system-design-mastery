## How To Use This Section

Practice is **deliberate**, not passive. The temptation is to read the case studies and feel productive. Resist that. Real practice looks like:

1. **Flashcards daily** — 10 minutes, spaced repetition. Use Anki or similar. The deck is provided as JSON.
2. **Mock interviews weekly** — schedule them. Use the rubric to score yourself or have a peer score you. Track trends over weeks.
3. **Review checklists** before you submit any architecture artifact (ADR, RFC, design doc). They're short on purpose.
4. **Case studies** — read with a notebook open. After each one, write 2-3 lessons that apply to _your_ current system.

---

## The Discipline of Practice

Reading the course once will not transform you. Practice 30 minutes a day for 6 months _will_. The math is simple:

> 30 min/day × 180 days = **90 hours of deliberate practice**.
>
> Most engineers do 0 hours/year of deliberate architecture practice. You'll be in the top 5% just by showing up.

The case studies are the highest-leverage section. Real outages, especially ones with public post-mortems, contain more lessons per page than any textbook. Make them part of your weekly diet.
